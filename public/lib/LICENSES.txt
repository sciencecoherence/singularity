  Refineries: Wikipedia, "List of oil refineries" (CC BY-SA 4.0), retrieved 11 October 2026.
  Positions: OpenStreetMap contributors via Nominatim (ODbL 1.0).
  AI data centres: Epoch AI, Frontier Data Centers hub (CC BY 4.0).
  The incident register is original, compiled from the sources each entry links.


Solar page images (loaded live, not bundled):
  SDO/HMI and SDO/AIA courtesy of NASA/SDO and the AIA and HMI science teams, served by Helioviewer.org (ESA/NASA).
  GOES-19 SUVI, SOHO LASCO C3 (ESA/NASA) and WSA-Enlil images via NOAA SWPC (public domain).

countries-data.js — data, not code:
  Countries, names, demonyms: mledoze/countries (ODbL 1.0).
  Electricity mix, oil and gas production and consumption: Our World in Data, energy dataset (CC BY 4.0).

war-data.js — data, not code:
  Ongoing armed conflicts, their places and death tallies: Wikipedia, List of ongoing armed
  conflicts, 13 October 2026 (CC BY-SA 4.0). The app reads the live page; this copy stands in until it answers.

currency-data.js — data, not code:
  Each country's main currency (ISO 4217 code, name, symbol): mledoze/countries (ODbL 1.0).
